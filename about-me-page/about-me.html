About Me

Get to Know Me!

Hey! I’m Muhais , a sophomore at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign majoring in Statistics with minors in Data Science and Business. I’m curious about how we make decisions, from what drives digital products to how data shapes behavior.

When I’m not working on a new side project or debugging code, you’ll probably catch me playing Fortnite or managing my squad in EA FC. I'm a Gunner, so you know I support Arsenal ⚽.

My favorite food is semo and efo riro with turkey 🍽️, a dish rich in flavor and history. As a Muslim and proud Nigerian 🇳🇬, my faith and culture guide everything I pursue.

These days, I'm exploring how to build smarter tools that streamline tasks. You can also find me sharing insights and reflections on LinkedIn or creating content on TikTok. Feel free to reach out—let's connect!
