About Me
01 INTRO
01 / 05

Hey, I’m Muhais.

I’m the type of person who can have one random thought, think about it for way too long, and eventually decide, “I might as well do something with this.”

A lot of what interests me sits somewhere between data, technology, business, creativity, and people. I like figuring out why things work the way they do, and sometimes questioning why they were made that way in the first place.

02 BUILD
02 / 05

My ideas rarely stay ideas.

A lot of my projects start with one question: “Why doesn’t this already exist?”

Sometimes the answer becomes a website. Sometimes it becomes a data project, an app concept, or something sitting unfinished in my notes. Either way, building things is one of the main ways I learn.
